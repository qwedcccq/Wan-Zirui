# Method families and sources

Papers you can draw on when implementing your methods.  Each entry names a
method family (filtering, artifact handling, spatial filtering, statistics);
the implementation and its parameters are yours to choose.

## Filtering and referencing

- Widmann, Schröger & Maess (2015). Digital filter design for electrophysiological
  data: a practical approach. *J. Neurosci. Methods* 250, 34–46.
- Ferdjallah & Barr (1994). Adaptive digital notch filter design on the unit circle
  for the removal of powerline noise. *IEEE Trans. Biomed. Eng.* 41, 529–536.
- Donoho & Johnstone (1994). Ideal spatial adaptation by wavelet shrinkage.
  *Biometrika* 81, 425–455.
- Ludwig et al. (2009). Using a common average reference to improve cortical
  neuron recordings from microelectrode arrays. *J. Neurophysiol.* 101, 1679–1689.
- Yao et al. (2019). Which reference should we use for EEG and ERP practice?
  *Brain Topogr.* 32, 530–549.

## Artifact handling

- Urigüen & Garcia-Zapirain (2015). EEG artifact removal — state-of-the-art and
  guidelines. *J. Neural Eng.* 12, 031001.
- Mumtaz, Rasheed & Irfan (2021). Review of challenges associated with the EEG
  artifact removal methods. *Biomed. Signal Process. Control* 68, 102741.
- Perrin et al. (1989). Spherical splines for scalp potential and current density
  mapping. *Electroencephalogr. Clin. Neurophysiol.* 72, 184–187.
- Delorme & Makeig (2004). EEGLAB: an open source toolbox for analysis of
  single-trial EEG dynamics including independent component analysis.
  *J. Neurosci. Methods* 134, 9–21.
- Bigdely-Shamlo et al. (2015). The PREP pipeline: standardized preprocessing for
  large-scale EEG analysis. *Front. Neuroinform.* 9, 16.
- Nolan, Whelan & Reilly (2010). FASTER: fully automated statistical thresholding
  for EEG artifact rejection. *J. Neurosci. Methods* 192, 152–162.
- Fitzgibbon et al. (2016). Automatic determination of EMG-contaminated components
  and validation of independent component analysis. *Clin. Neurophysiol.* 127,
  1781–1793.
- Pope et al. (2022). Managing electromyogram contamination in scalp recordings.
  *Brain Behav.* 12, e2721.

## Spatial filtering and decoding

- Ramoser, Müller-Gerking & Pfurtscheller (2000). Optimal spatial filtering of
  single trial EEG during imagined hand movement. *IEEE Trans. Rehabil. Eng.* 8,
  441–446.
- Ang et al. (2008). Filter bank common spatial pattern (FBCSP) in brain–computer
  interface. *Proc. IJCNN*, 2390–2397.
- Ma et al. (2023). Transformed common spatial pattern for motor imagery-based
  brain–computer interfaces. *Front. Neurosci.* 17, 1116721.
- Cao et al. (2025). Improved filter bank common spatial pattern algorithm based on
  the sparrow search algorithm. *Front. Hum. Neurosci.* 19, 1679329.
- Gramfort et al. (2013). MEG and EEG data analysis with MNE-Python.
  *Front. Neurosci.* 7, 267.
